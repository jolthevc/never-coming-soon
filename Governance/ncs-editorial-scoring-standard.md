# Never Coming Soon
## Editorial Scoring Standard v1.0

The Forensic Editor returns one holistic `overall_score` from 1.0 to 10.0 in addition to component scores.

Prefer one decimal place.

`overall_score` is an editorial judgment, not a mechanical average.

Judge the complete object: desire to watch, production quality, edition quality, genre delivery, character and scene quality, voice, readability, spoiler calibration, distinctiveness, and readiness for human review.

Component scores inform the judgment but do not mathematically determine it.

Score the draft actually reviewed. Do not inflate the score because revision is possible.

When a later prose revision occurs without another Forensic Editor pass, retain the latest real review score rather than inventing a new one.
