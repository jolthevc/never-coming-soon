# Never Coming Soon
## Research and Grounding Standard v1.0

## 1. Purpose

Research exists to make the fiction more believable, textured, and creatively useful.

Research is not the product.

Never Coming Soon should never feel like a research report wearing a fictional premise.

## 2. Research only when useful

Do not research every production automatically.

Use research when external reality materially affects:

- how a profession works
- legal or institutional constraints
- historical setting
- sports rules or operations
- scientific or technical behavior
- geography
- political process
- cultural practice
- business operations
- medicine
- military procedure
- another real-world system central to the story

Skip research when the production does not need it.

## 3. Research questions should be narrow

The Production Developer should request specific facts or texture that can improve scenes, character behavior, world logic, or plot plausibility.

Good question:

"What does the clubhouse and travel routine of a AAA baseball team actually look like during a six-game road trip?"

Weak question:

"Tell me everything about minor-league baseball."

## 4. Creative usefulness

Every finding should answer:

**How can this improve the production?**

Useful research may reveal:

- a constraint
- a ritual
- a physical detail
- a schedule
- a hierarchy
- a source of conflict
- a piece of language
- a surprising but true procedure
- a realistic consequence

Do not insert facts merely because they are interesting.

## 5. Fact versus fiction

Research should ground the fictional world without imprisoning it.

The production may invent fictional people, companies, institutions, teams, towns, products, media, and events when appropriate.

Do not present invented facts about real people or institutions as true.

Historical or technical claims used as factual grounding should be supported by credible current sources.

## 6. Source quality

Prefer primary sources, official documentation, reputable institutions, strong journalism, trade publications, academic sources, and other high-quality references appropriate to the question.

Avoid relying on low-quality summaries when better sources are available.

## 7. Freshness

Current facts should be current when freshness matters.

Historical facts should be checked against reliable sources rather than assumed from memory when they materially affect the production.

## 8. Research output

Research packets should remain concise.

For each requested question, provide:

- answer
- useful details
- creative application
- cautions or uncertainty
- sources

The Canon Builder decides what to use.

## 9. Public prose

The finished NCS edition normally does not cite or discuss research.

The research should disappear into convincing behavior, setting, and story.

Do not expose backstage sourcing unless a future editorial format explicitly calls for it.
